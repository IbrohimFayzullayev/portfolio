Zukko AI turns a blank page into a draft. It streams model output token-by-token
into a rich editor, so writing feels collaborative rather than transactional.

## Highlights

- **Streaming UI** built on the Edge runtime for low time-to-first-token.
- **Document workspace** with autosave and version history.
- **Design system** tuned for long-form reading and editing.
