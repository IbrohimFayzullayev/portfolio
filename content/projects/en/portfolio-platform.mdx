The site you're reading. Built as a long-lived foundation rather than a one-off,
with a typed content layer, i18n and a custom design system.

## Architecture

- **Content as data** via Velite — MDX compiled and type-checked at build time.
- **i18n** with `next-intl` and locale-prefixed routing.
- **SEO** baked in: dynamic OG images, RSS, sitemap and structured data.
