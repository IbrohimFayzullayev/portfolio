Radius CRM is a sales workspace where speed is the feature. The goal was an
interface that feels instant even with thousands of records on screen.

## Highlights

- **Real-time pipelines** over WebSocket with optimistic updates.
- **Virtualized tables** that stay smooth at 10k+ rows.
- **Command palette** for keyboard-first navigation.

## What I worked on

I owned the front-end architecture: data-fetching patterns, the component library,
and the performance budget that kept interactions under 100ms.
